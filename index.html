Customer Self-Service Portal

Evinova (AstraZeneca) · Associate Director, Product · June 2025 – Present

600 → 1,350 MAU

Problem

Evinova's global pharma and clinical trial clients had no way to submit a request, check on one, or find an answer without calling or emailing support — costly on two fronts, since it strained CSAT and support capacity, and every call carried compliance weight in Evinova's GXP-regulated environment.

Approach

Started from the vision, not a feature list — ran ideation and scoping sessions with stakeholders, then pressure-tested it with client interviews via Sales and a scan of comparable service portals. That narrowed a broad vision into a scoped 0-to-1 plan: PRDs, design specs with the design team, and a phased roadmap coordinated with Engineering across sprints.

Impact

  • Grew from 600 to 1,350 MAU since first release
  • Improved CSAT from 60 to 72
  • Reduced inbound support calls by 31%
  • Onboarding no longer requires scaling support headcount in lockstep

Stakeholder challenge

My hardest stakeholder was the head of Customer Support, who feared the portal would either offload work onto her team or underdeliver and make CS look bad. I brought her into discovery early, and when she pushed for per-product form customization — four forms to maintain — I built one form that captured everything she needed instead, with overlapping fields merged. The 31% drop in support calls after launch turned her from skeptic into advocate.

Shipped in increments, not one big release

  • Nov 2025Case submission form & basic knowledge base — first release, ~5 months from kickoff
  • Feb 2026Case notifications & feedback form
  • Mar 2026Single sign-on
  • Apr 2026Case submission form enhancements
  • May 2026Knowledge base restructured by product

Engagement & Retention Across eCommerce, CRM & Payments

Texada Software · Product Manager · Jan 2023 – Feb 2025

Churn −5% in one quarter

Problem

A B2B SaaS platform spanning eCommerce, CRM, payments, and rental mobile — serving 400+ customers across North America and APAC — needed clearer visibility into where customers were losing engagement and why.

Approach

Used PostHog analytics and structured A/B testing to find friction points in the product, then shipped AI-driven features — including damage detection and automated product descriptions — to make workflows feel smarter and less manual. Launched two new products from 0 to full rollout with a distributed team spanning Canada and APAC.

Impact

  • Reduced churn by 5% in one quarter
  • Grew MAU by 7%
  • Improved engagement by 15% via AI-driven features
  • Improved new feature adoption by 20%

Patient Experience for Decentralized Clinical Trials

ClinOne · Product Manager, Patient Experience · March 2022 – Nov 2022

2,000+ global sites

Problem

A decentralized clinical trial platform needed to deliver a consistent, high-quality patient and care-coordinator experience across 2,000+ trial sites worldwide — despite major cultural and operational differences in how people used it.

Approach

Ran 28 structured Voice of Customer sessions with patients and care coordinators to understand real usage, then designed and ran A/B tests to validate changes with behavioral evidence rather than assumptions — in close partnership with Design and Engineering.

Impact

  • Cut user complaints by 17% within three months
  • Improved patient portal usability by 25%
  • Delivered 97% of feature deadlines on time
